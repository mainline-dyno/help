What decides the PCD

    Make and modelTwins share hubs: BT-50 (to 2020) with Ranger, BT-50 (2020+) with D-Max.

    Year and generationSeries changes move PCD. LandCruiser 300 went back to 6-stud from the 200's 5x150.
  • 3
    Drivetrain and ride height2WD Hilux Workmate runs 5x114.3; 4WD and Hi-Rider run 6x139.7.
  • 4
    MarketGrey imports and other markets can differ from Australian delivery.

    Confirm on the vehicleCount studs and measure the PCD before ordering an adapter.
