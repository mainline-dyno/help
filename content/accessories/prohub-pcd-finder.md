The Mainline ProHub PCD Finder looks up the bolt pattern (PCD), centre bore and stud thread for a vehicle, and suggests the Mainline ProHub axle hub adapter that suits it. The specs are for Australian-market vehicles, and every result has a link to check it on wheel-size.com.

## Using the finder

**Find PCD** Choose the make, model and year (build date). The result shows the PCD, centre bore and thread, and the suggested ProHub adapter part number.

**ProHub adapters** Filter the Mainline ProHub axle hub adapter list by part number, PCD or vehicle.

**All vehicles** Filter every vehicle in the finder by make, model, series, PCD or part number.

**Vehicle not listed?** Type any make, model and year to open that vehicle's page on wheel-size.com.

Changeover years can show two generations. Use the compliance plate or build date to pick the right one.

## What decides the PCD

**Make and model** Twins share hubs: BT-50 (to 2020) with Ranger, BT-50 (2020+) with D-Max.

**Year and generation** Series changes move PCD. LandCruiser 300 went back to 6-stud from the 200's 5x150.

**Drivetrain and ride height** 2WD Hilux Workmate runs 5x114.3; 4WD and Hi-Rider run 6x139.7.

**Market** Grey imports and other markets can differ from Australian delivery.

## Verified and Check results

**Verified** The PCD, centre bore and thread were cross-checked against published fitment data.

**Check** The PCD is standard for that series, but the centre bore and thread should be confirmed before machining.

{{% notice warning %}}
Always confirm measurements before ordering. The information in this tool is a guide only, compiled from published fitment data, and may contain errors or omissions. Specifications can vary by build date, market, variant, and aftermarket or previous modifications. Count the studs and confirm the PCD, centre bore, thread size and clearance on the vehicle, and confirm the adapter with Mainline Dyno, before ordering or fitting.
